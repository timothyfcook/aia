AI Attribution (AIA)

AIA is an attribution system for writing that helps readers understand a writer's intent. It works on two axes—ideas and words—to indicate the level of AI vs. human contribution. The goal is to give us all an honest framework for how much attention our writing deserves. Read the post for details. This is v1, send me feedback @timothyfcook.
